# The darkness is taking over the rainbow, they must be stopped!

![Level1 screenshot](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nicklaslof/rainbow-ricochet/refs/heads/main/screenshots/screenshot1.png)
Hunt down the darkness in four action packed levels and save the rainbow to be overtaken by darkness.

## Controls
### A classic FPS controlled game. Mouse instead of trackpad is recommended.

<kbd>W</kbd> <kbd>A</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd> <kbd>D</kbd> or arrow keys - To move around

Mouse - To look around

<kbd>E</kbd> - To show the map

Left mouse button - Fire the unicorn horn (After you have found it)

Right mouse button - Throw the rainbow (After you have found it)

**Mouse sensitivity** can be adjusted by pressing <kbd>+</kbd> or <kbd>-</kbd>

You can **run out of bullets** but you will find new ones around each level.

The rainbow has a **two second cooldown** after catching it until you can throw it again.

Level progress is saved so you can close the browser and come back later to continue on the level you last played.

Mostly no AI was harmed during the production of this game.

Game thumbnail and cover image created in Blender.
